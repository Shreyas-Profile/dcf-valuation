About us

Arush PinisettiCo-founder · Models

Hi, my name is Arush! I am an aspiring finance professional who is actively preparing for a career in investment banking. I initially wanted to build this app because I always found that the number of resources to explore intrinsic valuation were scarce, and the very few options often isolated those who are new to the concepts. I co-founded this app to allow people to explore DCF analysis further and actually understand why we make certain assumptions and decisions. My role in this process was to build the base case models which you see on the app, and come up with justifications for my metrics. In my spare time I enjoy probing the financial world by writing blogs and building models.

Shreyas PavuluriCo-founder · Website

Hi, my name is Shreyas! I am an aspiring mechanical engineer with a strong interest in quantum computing and artificial intelligence, and I have completed a number of projects in both fields. I joined this project to extend my experience into finance and broaden my portfolio, and in doing so I gained a deeper understanding of how companies are valued and why each assumption is significant. I designed and built the website and its interactive features, including the assumption sliders, the macroeconomic scenarios, the AI assistant and the saved valuations. Arush developed the financial models in Excel, and my role was to transform them into an accessible application for anyone, regardless of their prior knowledge of finance.

A learning project, not financial advice. Market data from Yahoo Finance (delayed).
