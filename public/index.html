About us

A
Arush PinisettiCo-founder · Models

Hi, my name is Arush! I am an aspiring finance professional who is actively preparing for a career in investment banking. I initially wanted to build this app because I always found that the number of resources to explore intrinsic valuation were scarce, and the very few options often isolated those who are new to the concepts. I co-founded this app to allow people to explore DCF analysis further and actually understand why we make certain assumptions and decisions. My role in this process was to build the base case models which you see on the app, and come up with justifications for my metrics. In my spare time I enjoy probing the financial world by writing blogs and building models.
