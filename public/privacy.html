Privacy and terms

Last updated 21 November 2026

DCF Sandbox (dcf.shreyas.uk) is a free learning project made by Shreyas Pavuluri and Arush Pinisetti. This page explains what we store and the rules for using the site.

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We use these only to run your account.
  • Valuations you save: the company, the slider settings, the macro scenario you picked, the resulting values and the name you give it.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in on that device for up to 30 days. Signing out deletes it.
  • On your device only: your current slider settings and chat history are kept in your browser so the app remembers them. They aren't sent to us unless you save a valuation or ask the AI a question.

The AI helper

When you use "Ask AI", your question and a summary of the numbers on your screen are sent to an AI model (through OpenRouter) to write the answer. Don't type personal information into it. We don't keep your chat on our servers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We only ask Google for your basic profile and email, nothing else from your Google account.

Where it's kept

Accounts and saved valuations are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site. Market prices come from Yahoo Finance.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved valuation from the home page. To delete your whole account, email the address shown on the Google sign-in screen and we'll remove everything.

Terms of use

  • This site is for learning only. It is not financial advice, and nothing on it tells you to buy or sell anything.
  • The models and AI answers can be wrong. Market data may be delayed or missing.
  • Please don't misuse the site, for example by trying to break it or overload it.
  • The site is provided as it is, without guarantees, and may change or stop at any time.
